## Planned outline

1. **What "discovery" means.** Finding a new question, proposing a new model, and proving or testing a result are different activities.
2. **Where AI already helps.** Searching and summarising literature, writing and debugging code, checking algebra, exploring parameter spaces.
3. **Where it is unreliable.** Confident but wrong derivations, invented references, and results that are plausible but untested.
4. **Verification as the core skill.** Symbolic checks, numerical tests and limiting cases as the way to turn a suggestion into knowledge.
5. **An open question.** Whether these tools can originate genuinely new scientific ideas is not settled. The article will set out arguments on both sides rather than give a verdict.
