## Planned outline

1. **The continuum hypothesis.** Fields such as displacement and stress are defined at every point, which assumes the material looks smooth at the scale of interest.
2. **Signs of trouble.** Size-dependent stiffness, surface-dominated behaviour, and deformation concentrated over a few atomic spacings.
3. **Extended continua.** Surface elasticity, strain-gradient and nonlocal theories: each introduces a length scale that classical elasticity does not have.
4. **Checking the extensions.** Comparisons with atomistic simulations and experiments, and why agreement in one problem does not guarantee agreement in another.
5. **A working view.** The article will argue for a position, clearly marked as personal interpretation.

<Callout kind="interpretation">
Planned thesis: continuum models remain the most useful language for engineering at small scales, provided their additional parameters are identified carefully and their range of validity is stated.
</Callout>
