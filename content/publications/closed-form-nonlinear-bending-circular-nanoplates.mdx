This work develops a closed-form approximation for the nonlinear bending of clamped circular nanoplates under uniform pressure, incorporating surface effects through a modified Gurtin–Murdoch model within the Föppl–von Kármán framework.

The analytical model is validated against finite-difference solutions and finite-element simulations, with a maximum deviation of 2.3% over the investigated parameter ranges.
