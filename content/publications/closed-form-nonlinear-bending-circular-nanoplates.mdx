This article gives an analytical pressure–deflection relation for circular thin nanoplates in which surface effects matter. It combines a modified Gurtin–Murdoch surface elasticity theory with the Föppl–von Kármán plate equations, and a hybrid Galerkin–collocation approach leads to a fifth-order polynomial. Compared with finite-difference and finite-element solutions, the formula stays within 2.3% over the materials, aspect ratios and surface tensions studied. The analysis also brings out a net membrane force, not caused by strain, that adds a linear term to the response and delays the onset of nonlinearity, and it gives a displacement profile that changes with pressure. The model is meant as a practical tool for designing nano-electro-mechanical systems and interpreting bulge tests.
