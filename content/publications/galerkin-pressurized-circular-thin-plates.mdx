This chapter proposes a simple formula linking pressure to central deflection for clamped circular thin plates undergoing large deflections. Galerkin’s method is applied with a new displacement field containing two unknown parameters, which gives the load–deflection relation as a polynomial fraction. Compared with Timoshenko’s model and a finite-difference solution, the formula reproduces the load–deflection curve with very high precision and closely matches the deformed shape of the plate. Unlike Timoshenko’s model, the resulting radial displacement does not violate the in-plane equilibrium equation.
