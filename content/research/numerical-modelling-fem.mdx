## Why it matters

A closed-form model is only as useful as the evidence that it is right. Finite-element simulations provide an independent check, provided they are themselves trustworthy: converged meshes, appropriate elements, and boundary conditions that really correspond to the analytical problem.

## Focus

- Modelling surface effects in commercial finite-element software, where they are not built in.
- Mesh and load-step convergence studies for geometrically nonlinear problems.
- Reusable MATLAB and Python scripts for post-processing and comparison with analytical results. Some of these will appear in the [Computational Lab](/lab/).
