## Background

<Callout kind="established">
A shallow spherical cap under external pressure can follow an equilibrium path with a **limit point**: beyond a critical load, no nearby equilibrium exists and the cap jumps, or *snaps through*, to an inverted configuration. Classical shell theory predicts this behaviour, and real shells are known to be highly sensitive to imperfections.
</Callout>

The [two-bar truss demonstration](/lab/von-mises-truss/) in the Computational Lab shows the same mechanism in its simplest possible form.

## Aims

- Formulate the equilibrium of thin spherical caps with geometric nonlinearity.
- Locate limit points and follow equilibrium paths through them.
- Investigate whether, and how, surface elasticity modifies the stability of very thin shells.

## Methods

Analytical reduction where possible, numerical continuation methods to follow equilibrium paths, and finite-element models to check both.
