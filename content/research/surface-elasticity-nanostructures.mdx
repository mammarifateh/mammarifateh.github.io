## Questions

- How do residual surface stress and surface stiffness each contribute to the apparent stiffness of a thin nanostructure?
- Which structural problems (plates, shells, beams) keep a closed-form or nearly closed-form solution once surface terms are added?
- At what length scales does a continuum description with surface elasticity remain a reasonable model, and when does it need to be compared with atomistic results?
